About Me

I'm Shreeya Jangid, a clinical neuropsychiatrist and people strategist from India, now pursuing my MSc in Clinical Neuropsychiatry at the University of Birmingham.

I'm fascinated by how the brain shapes behavior and performance—especially in high-stakes environments. My research focuses on executive dysfunction following mild traumatic brain injury and its impact on emotional regulation and workplace functioning.

Beyond academia, I'm passionate about luxury strategy, motorsports, HR operations, and the intersection of neuroscience with organizational excellence. I believe in advocating for people as passionately as I pursue research.

Research & Projects

Ongoing • MSc Dissertation

Executive Dysfunction Following Mild Traumatic Brain Injury

MSc Clinical Neuropsychiatry, University of Birmingham • Expected Submission: August 2025

A systematic literature review examining how executive dysfunction arising from mild TBI cascades into disrupted emotional regulation and impaired occupational functioning. Synthesises neuropsychiatric and occupational health literature to surface clinical and workplace implications—bridging brain injury science with real-world recovery pathways for working adults.

mTBI Executive Function Emotional Regulation Occupational Health Systematic Review
Jan – Apr 2024

Environmental Perspectives: Attitudes, Values & Self-Control

Manipal University Jaipur, India

Explored the relationship between environmental attitudes, personal values, and self-control among BBA and BCom students using mixed-methods approach. Utilized the Self-Control Scale (SCS), Environmental Values Questionnaire (EPVQ), and Environmental Attitude Inventory (EAI) with descriptive statistics, correlation, and t-test analyses. Revealed how psychological factors interact in environmental awareness and pro-environmental behaviour contexts.

Environmental Psychology Self-Control Mixed Methods Quantitative Analysis Values & Attitudes
