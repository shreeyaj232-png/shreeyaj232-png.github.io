About Me

I'm Shreeya Jangid, a clinical neuropsychiatrist and people strategist from India, now pursuing my MSc in Clinical Neuropsychiatry at the University of Birmingham. I'm fascinated by how the brain shapes behavior, performance, and wellbeing—especially in high-stakes environments.

My academic focus centers on executive dysfunction following mild traumatic brain injury and its impact on emotional regulation and occupational functioning. Beyond research, I'm equally passionate about HR strategy, client experience, and the intersection of neuroscience with luxury and motorsports industries.

As an elected Student Representative and Ambassador, I advocate for people as passionately as I pursue research. Fluent in English and Hindi, currently learning French.

Research

MSc Dissertation

Executive Dysfunction Following mTBI

University of Birmingham · Submission: August 2025

A systematic literature review examining how executive dysfunction arising from mild TBI cascades into disrupted emotional regulation and impaired occupational functioning. Synthesises neuropsychiatric and occupational health literature to surface clinical and workplace implications — bridging brain injury science with real-world recovery pathways for working adults.

mTBI Executive Function Emotional Regulation Occupational Health

Environmental Perspectives: Attitudes, Values & Self-Control

Manipal University Jaipur

Explored the relationship between environmental attitudes, personal values, and self-control among BBA and BCom students. Mixed-methods approach using the Self-Control Scale (SCS), Environmental Values Questionnaire (EPVQ), and Environmental Attitude Inventory (EAI). Descriptive statistics, correlation, and t-test analyses revealed how psychological factors interact in environmental awareness and pro-environmental behaviour contexts.
