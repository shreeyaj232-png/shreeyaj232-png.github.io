About Me

I'm Shreeya Jangid, a clinical neuropsychiatrist and people strategist from India, now pursuing my MSc in Clinical Neuropsychiatry at the University of Birmingham. I'm fascinated by how the brain shapes behavior and performance in high-stakes environments.

My research focuses on executive dysfunction following mild traumatic brain injury and its occupational impact. Beyond academia, I'm passionate about luxury strategy, motorsports, HR operations, and building high-performance teams.

As an elected Student Representative and Ambassador, I advocate for people as passionately as I pursue research. Fluent in English and Hindi, currently learning French.
