I spent four years studying why people do the things they do, then decided the most interesting lab was an office. A psychology degree turns out to be excellent training for HR. You already know when someone's "no red flags" is doing a lot of quiet work.

A paddock, a boardroom, a hiring pipeline. They all reward the same thing.
