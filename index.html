The Philosophy

Form
Follows
Function.

Grid as Grammar

Every module is dimensioned to a strict 40cm grid. Desks align with shelves. Shelves align with cabinets. The result is a workspace that looks inevitable, not accidental.

Material Honesty

Bare steel frames. Exposed birch ply. Powder-coated in the original Bauhaus primaries. No veneers, no laminates — the material is the aesthetic.

Purpose in Every Joint

Connections are visible because visibility enables understanding. You can see how your desk is held together, and that knowledge builds confidence and ownership of your space.

Infinitely Modular

Start with a single desk module. Add a shelf tower. Integrate a drawer cabinet. The system scales without friction because every unit is designed as part of a greater whole.

"A workspace where nothing is arbitrary,
everything is earned."

The Assembly

Precision
in Four Steps.

01

Select Your Modules

Choose from our catalogue. Our online configurator shows exactly how each module combines in real time. No guesswork.

02

Delivered Flat

Every component ships in a purpose-built flat-pack. Components are pre-drilled. Hardware is pre-sorted in numbered bags.

03

Bolt It Together

One Allen key. All connections are M8 bolts into threaded inserts. The process takes under 45 minutes for a full desk system.

04

Ready to Work

Your space is complete. And when you need to evolve it — add a module, remove one, reconfigure — the system accommodates it in minutes.

Note

All modules ship with a precision-printed assembly drawing — isometric, dimensioned, numbered. No app required. No QR codes. Just a diagram that works.
